Stir-fry Beef and Broccoli

A quick and easy stir-fry recipe. This recipe serves 4 with a total preparation time of 60 mins.

Ingredients:

  • 80 mL oyster sauce
  • 80 mL cooking sherry
  • 10 mL toasted sesame oil
  • 5 mL soy sauce
  • 5 g white sugar
  • 3 g cornflour
  • 340 g beef round steak, thinly sliced
  • 60 mL vegetable oil, plus extra if needed
  • 1 thin slice fresh ginger
  • 1 garlic clove, peeled and crushed
  • 450 g broccoli, cut into florets

Steps

  1. Gather and prepare all of the ingredients.
  2. Whisk together the oyster sauce, cooking sherry, sesame oil, soy sauce, sugar and cornflour until the sugar has dissolved.
  3. Place the sliced beef in a shallow bowl and pour the sauce mixture over it. Stir until coated, cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil in a wok or large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for about 1 minute to flavour the oil, then remove them.
  5. Add the broccoli and stir-fry for 5–7 minutes, until bright green and nearly tender. Remove the broccoli from the wok and set aside.
  6. Add a little more oil if necessary, then add the beef together with its marinade.
  7. Stir-fry for about 5 minutes, until the beef is cooked through and the sauce has thickened and become glossy.
  8. Return the broccoli to the wok and stir everything together for approximately 3 minutes, or until heated through.
  9. Serve immediately, preferably with rice.
